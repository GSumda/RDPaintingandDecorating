About Us

With over 15 years of experience in the trade, RD Painting & Property Maintenance was founded on the values of quality craftsmanship and honest, reliable service. Our journey began with a painting apprenticeship completed in 2008, and since then we’ve grown into a trusted name for all aspects of painting, decorating, and property maintenance across both residential and commercial properties. Whether you’re refreshing a single room, transforming a full interior, or protecting and maintaining your exterior, we cover it all. We work alongside established property developers, interior designers, and commercial contractors, and are fully insured for residential, commercial, and exterior work — giving you confidence that the job will be done properly, on time, and to a high standard.
